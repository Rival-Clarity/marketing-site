GOOD QUESTIONS.

A few things
to know.

A straightforward service starts with straightforward answers.

What exactly does Rival Clarity track?

The proposed first version follows supported public pricing pages, product pages, changelogs, and blogs. It looks for price changes, product launches, and shifts in messaging. Coverage depends on the pages available for each competitor.

How is this different from checking the websites myself?

The service is designed to compare observations over time and bring the relevant changes together in a short brief. You can still inspect the original sources; Rival Clarity helps make keeping up a repeatable routine.

Will I get an email if nothing important changed?

The proposed weekly brief goes out when there is something useful to share. A quiet week can stay quiet. The service should never invent a finding to fill an email.

Can I choose or change my competitors?

Yes. The proposed plans let you choose the companies on your watchlist. A new competitor needs an initial baseline before changes can be detected over time.

What happens after I sign up?

The intended flow is to confirm your email, check whether the competitor’s public pages are supported, establish a baseline, and then look for changes. This mockup demonstrates that experience; it does not create an account or start monitoring.
