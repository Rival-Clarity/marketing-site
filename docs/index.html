GOOD QUESTIONS.

A few things
to know.

A straightforward service starts with straightforward answers.

What happens after I create an account?

Sign up in the Rival Clarity app, then enter your business website. Choose from sourced competitor suggestions or add your own. Open company profiles for research, check the public pages you choose, and explore search, reviews, and social posts as needed.

Does Rival Clarity cover every page and social network?

Coverage depends on accessible public sources and the pages you choose to watch. Recent-post collection currently supports X. LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook links identify official profiles; they do not include post collection. Reviews from G2 and Capterra depend on readable, verified public profiles.

How do I know what is fact and what is interpretation?

Research and findings include sources, captured evidence, and dates. AI interpretations are labeled separately. Missing information stays unknown, and a missing search term is only missing from the saved sample. Check the evidence before acting.

Will I automatically start receiving emails?

No. Your brief is currently available in the app to read, copy, and download. Optional weekly email is coming next. It will require a separate opt-in with your verified account email and include unsubscribe controls.

Can I change the competitors I follow?

Yes. Add or remove competitors from your watchlist. Removing a company turns off your daily checks and hides its findings while retaining collected research and saved evidence. Explicitly re-adding it restores previous research; paused page checks can be enabled again.

Do I need a credit card to get started?

No credit card is required to create an account. Rival Clarity is an early product with collection limits. There is no paid checkout or active $19 Founder subscription; any future paid offer will explain its price and terms before you purchase.
