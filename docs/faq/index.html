How can a small business track competitor pricing changes without checking websites every week?

In Rival Clarity, add a competitor’s public pricing page to your watchlist and turn on daily checks. You can then review changes together in your weekly brief. To set up the first page:

  1. Choose a competitor your customers compare you with. In its profile, open the pages you’re watching and add its pricing URL if it isn’t already listed.
  2. Select Check now. The first successful check saves a copy of the page text for future comparisons.
  3. Turn on Daily checks for that page. When a later check finds a meaningful change, open the finding to see what the page said before and after.

Which competitor pages should I watch first?

Start with the pricing or services page a buyer would open to compare your offer. If buyers ask why a feature costs extra, watch the plan comparison page, too. If a competitor often launches a competing feature, add its product updates page.

Choose a public URL that shows the relevant text. A price available only after signing in or requesting a quote is outside the coverage of a public page check.

When will I see the first change?

The first successful check establishes a baseline. A finding can appear when a later successful check captures a meaningful difference. If the pricing stays the same, you may have several observations before you see a finding. If a check fails, the page status shows that it needs attention.

Daily checks capture the page at particular times. A promotion that starts and ends between two checks can be missed.

How can I tell whether a competitor actually changed its price?

Open the finding and compare the dated excerpts. Check that both amounts refer to the same plan, currency, billing period, and unit. A page that switches from “per month” to “per month, billed annually” can look like a price cut even when the monthly plan stayed the same.

Rival Clarity keeps the captured wording and source link alongside its interpretation. Use the excerpts to establish what changed and the interpretation to decide what to investigate.

What goes into my weekly competitor brief?

The brief collects findings for the week from pages on your watchlist. It links back to sources so you can inspect a price change, a new product description, or a shift in how a competitor describes its offer. You can read, copy, or download the brief in the app.

What do I need to get started?

Create a Rival Clarity account and enter your own business website. Choose from the suggested competitors or add one you already know. You can start with one competitor and one page. Creating an account requires no credit card.
