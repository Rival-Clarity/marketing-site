The short version: Marketing-site demo forms stay in your browser tab. The separate application stores account and workspace information to provide competitor monitoring. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

1. What this covers

This policy describes how Rival Clarity ("we," "us," or "our") handles information through our marketing website, interactive demos, the application at app.rivalclarity.com, and communications with us. Third-party websites and services have their own privacy policies.

2. Information we handle

  • Website demos. Email addresses, competitor websites, and other entries in the marketing-site and product-demo forms are used within the current browser tab. Those forms do not send entries to us, create accounts, start monitoring, or send emails. Demo changes reset when the page reloads.
  • Application accounts. If you sign in to the separate application, our authentication provider handles the sign-in information you provide, such as your email address and profile details. We use an account identifier to associate you with your workspace.
  • Workspace and monitoring data. We store competitor names, public page URLs, monitoring settings, saved findings, page observations, change evidence, and analysis associated with the service. Public pages may themselves contain personal information.
  • Technical information. Our hosting and service providers may process IP addresses, browser and device information, request times, and diagnostic or security logs. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's data-collection notice.
  • Communications. If you email us, we receive your address, message, and any information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, provide and maintain workspaces, collect and compare public competitor pages, generate findings and summaries, respond to requests, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. The current marketing demos do not enroll you in marketing emails, and billing and email-brief delivery are not currently connected.

4. Providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate Rival Clarity, including GitHub Pages for the marketing website, Render for application hosting and storage, Clerk for authentication, Temporal Cloud for monitoring workflows, and OpenAI for AI-assisted interpretation. When AI analysis is enabled, selected excerpts from public-page change evidence are sent to OpenAI to generate possible implications and review suggestions.

The marketing website also loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to request those fonts and sends technical request information, including your IP address. See Google Fonts privacy information.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and security, or complete a merger or transfer of the business, subject to applicable protections. Providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate.

5. Cookies and tracking

The marketing website and static product demo do not set their own cookies, use persistent browser storage for form entries, or include advertising pixels or analytics scripts. The separate application uses authentication cookies and similar session technologies through Clerk to keep you signed in.

We do not track visitors across unrelated websites for advertising. Our website does not change its behavior in response to a browser's Do Not Track signal. Third-party providers may receive technical data as described above. You can control cookies through your browser; blocking authentication cookies may prevent application sign-in.

6. Retention and security

Marketing demo entries are temporary and are not stored by us. Application account, workspace, and monitoring records are retained to operate the service, maintain evidence, address support and security needs, and meet legal obligations. There is currently no fixed automatic deletion period for application records. Provider logs and backups may follow separate retention schedules.

We use safeguards such as encrypted connections and authenticated workspace access. No internet service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You can avoid entering information into demo forms, manage available application settings, or contact us at legal@rivalclarity.com to ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights. Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you additional rights. We may need to verify your identity before handling a request, and legal or security requirements may limit what we can delete.

Account deletion is not currently a self-service feature. Contact us for help; signing out or closing a browser tab does not delete application records.

8. Children

Rival Clarity is intended for adult business users, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will post the revised policy here, update the date above, and provide additional notice of material changes when required by law.

Privacy questions or requests: legal@rivalclarity.com.
